BuddyWalk is a safety-focused app designed to help college students feel more secure when walking alone, especially at night. With a simple and secure sign-up/login process, users can create a personalized profile featuring their name, college, and profile photo.

Leveraging the power of the Google Maps API, BuddyWalk offers a real-time map view displaying nearby students, making it easy to connect and coordinate walks home together via in-app messaging. The goal is to promote campus safety and foster a stronger sense of community among students.
